About NEFB Report

New England Football.
All in One Place.

NEFB Report is an independent media platform covering high school football across all six New England states and the region's prep football landscape.

Who We Are

Built for New England Football

NEFB Report was created to bring New England high school football coverage together in one place. From public-school programs to prep football, our goal is to make it easier for players, families, coaches and fans to follow football throughout the region.

We cover scores, rankings, recruiting, major matchups, player performances, team news and other stories from across New England.
